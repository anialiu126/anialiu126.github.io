I gave an invited talk, "Extracting physics from gravitational waves: parameter estimation and waveform modelling", at the <a href="https://aletorreso.wixsite.com/atogw/gwnext2026">GWnext 2026 Meeting</a>, held at the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA) on 2&ndash;6 March 2026. GWnext is a series of meetings on gravitational-wave physics from young researchers for young researchers, bringing together theory, astrophysics, and detection. My talk covered recent developments and challenges in the data analysis of gravitational-wave signals from the LIGO&ndash;Virgo&ndash;KAGRA Collaboration.
